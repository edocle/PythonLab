Privacy Policies
Every single company that collects user data must have a privacy policy outlining exactly which data they are collecting and how they use such data. This is part of the signup process, and it’s important that we read these documents carefully. If there’s anything in the policy that you disagree with, do not use the product.

Anonymous Data
In some cases, data must be stored in an anonymous or unidentifiable way. This is especially true with medical data, although there are companies that get around this by splitting the data and storing its pieces in different locations so that they can’t be linked together again easily. However, for data not as sensitive as this, companies must at least give their users the option to remain anonymous.

Data Disclosure
Companies must provide the conditions under which your data could be disclosed. For example, if the court subpoenas them during a criminal investigation, companies may have to divulge certain pieces of data or their entire database to the requesting party. Companies must make it clear in their privacy policies and terms the specific conditions where this is allowed.

Data Updates
Companies are obligated to keep their policies and user data up to date. Anytime there are any changes to terms and conditions or privacy policies, the companies must reissue those to all of their users and get their consent. Additionally, if there are any changes to user information, such as an address change or a phone number change, it’s up to the company to try to reflect these changes in their own databases as soon as they can as well.
